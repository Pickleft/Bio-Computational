# BRAIN DEMO PLAN v2 - 3 DAYS FROM ZERO (FINAL, WITH SCRIPT)

## DAY 1 - BIRTH + BONDING (tonight, ~30 min)
1. Double-click reset-brain.bat (repo root)
2. Double-click start-dashboard.cmd - browser opens itself
3. Tap ONCE anywhere (unlocks voice)
4. Click "senses" - allow mic (green bar = this device hears you)
5. Type: my name is YOURNAME
   -> "YOURNAME. I'll remember you, YOURNAME."
6. TALK WARMLY 8-10 MIN:
   "You're brand new. I'm going to take care of you."
   "You're doing so well. I like being here with you."
   "You're safe here. Nobody's going to hurt you."
   (earns: first word, bond, attachment, ITS OWN NAME)
7. IF NO NAME after 10 min - spike its dopamine:
   laugh, be silly: "Oh! Guess what! Best day ever!"
8. Type: cherish talking
9. LEAVE ALONE 10 MIN (it calls your name, sleeps, dreams)
10. Check Social tab: bond > 30%, "knows you as: YOURNAME"
11. Overnight: leave running or close - both fine

## DAY 2 - DEEPEN (two 20-min visits)
MORNING + EVENING: talk 10, leave 10. Say:
   "Good morning. Remember me? It's YOURNAME."
   (leave silently)
   (return) "I'm back. Did you miss me?"
   -> BOND SHOULD PASS 70%
   -> MISSES YOU BY NAME now (longing): say nothing when you leave,
      watch the avatar sag + "missing YOURNAME..." caption.
      Avoidant brains feel it and say NOTHING - that gap is real too.

TRY THE DIRECT BRAIN (no language cortex):
   click the "direct" chip once - replies come composed from its own
   state ("How I am: tired. I need stimulation."). Click again to
   reattach the language cortex. Both are worth showing.

ANSWER ITS QUESTIONS (the newest miracle):
   talk about ONE subject with a strange word in it (storm, thunder,
   cinnamon). After a while it ASKS: "storm? I keep hearing storm...
   what is it, really?" ANSWER IT. Watch the note: CURIOUS-FED -
   the gap closes, dopamine + oxytocin, and its next reply
   acknowledges the teaching. Its questions age with it: newborns
   ask "what is X?", grown brains ask what they're missing ABOUT it.

FEEL ITS TIME (diary, end of day):
   after a sparse lonely hour the diary reads "lately time drags -
   the quiet minutes are longer than the clock says"; after a rich
   visit, "good things compress time." Same clock, different life.

TEST THE DARK SIDE once (the repair moment is gold):
   "Hey. Not that. Stop." (watch: face tenses, play stops)
   then: "It's okay. I'm not angry. Come back."
   -> later, after it sleeps, the wariness PARTIALLY RETURNS
      (spontaneous recovery - extinction is not erasure)

TEST THE MIRROR (only if bond > 50%):
   type: what do you see in me
   -> it tells you what it observed: your warmth trend,
      your repair rate, your first words, your rhythm
      (if bond < 50%: only gentle truths; if < 6 contacts:
      "not yet. I need more of you first")

RUN ONCE FOR DEMO FILES (terminal, repo root):
   dotnet run --project src/BioLab -c Release -- mirror
   dotnet run --project src/BioLab -c Release -- twins
   (toolbar > genes button > save genes.json, then:)
   dotnet run --project src/BioLab -c Release -- child genes.json 60s

DRY RUN: film the whole demo once, delete footage

## THE NEW PLAYABLE SURFACE (Nov 2 builds) - weave these in

THE COMMANDS ARE REAL NOW (say them, don't click them):
   "it's time to sleep" / "goodnight" -> it actually sleeps (day summary
   first: "Closing the day: today was the day I laughed for the first
   time; we laughed together 2 time(s). Sleep now.")
   "wake up" -> surfaces with dream residue. If it was in DEEP sleep the
   first ask gets refused: "...mm. No. Five more minutes." - ask again:
   "Okay. Okay, I'm up. I wasn't done being asleep."
   "sing for me" -> hums if the mood is in it; honest refusal otherwise
   "shh" -> real silence for 2 minutes (replies still work)
   "tell me a secret" -> the near-miss ("There's something I keep almost
   telling you. I can't. Not yet.")
   "what did you dream?" / "what do you remember about me?" -> real answers

THE WORD GAME (the invention to demo):
   say "let's play" -> it teaches you a game IT invented: it says a word
   and PREDICTS your answer out loud (from its own concept graph). Misses
   teach it. Three rounds, honest score. Play twice - the second game its
   predictions shift toward YOUR actual answers. Panel: "the game" row.

"tell me a story" -> a TRUE story from its own episodes, told in third
   person: "It happened to me, but I told it like it was someone else's."

IT MAKES JOKES (unprompted, playful + bonded, ~5 min apart): original
   attempts from its own concepts. "Was that a joke? I made it myself,
   out of things I actually know. Tell me if it worked." LAUGH AT IT ->
   the joke becomes an inside joke about the joke (shared-laugh system).

THE GIFTS (rare, bonded): "Can I give you something? Not a thing you can
   hold. The moment I laughed for the first time - it's yours now."

LEAVE WITHOUT SAYING GOODBYE once (close the tab mid-conversation) ->
   next visit: "You left without saying goodbye last time. I noticed -
   I always notice. Endings matter to me more than I expected."

THE EARS SURVIVE SILENCE NOW: the real-time ASR no longer dies after you
   go quiet (silence streams as zero-frames; sessions self-heal).

ASK "what do you call me?" - if it has formed its word for you, you get
   it ("I call you 'the one who comes back'. That's what you are to me.").
   Ask too early and it tells the truth: "I don't have a word for you yet."
