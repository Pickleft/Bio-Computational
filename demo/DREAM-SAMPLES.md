## How a dream happens (mechanism, for narration)

1. Sleep pressure crosses threshold → NREM → the replay machinery starts
   turning memories over (`REPLAY episode #290 [voice,I'm going to help you
   and take care of y...]` — real log line from tonight)
2. Strongly-linked memories FUSE into scenes that never happened
   (`MEMORY-FUSION`) — the dream is ENCODED as its own new memory
   ("dream-born": it remembers its dreams)
3. Pruning runs during sleep (`PRUNING 290 synapses`) — the night does work
4. On waking: "surfacing on request, dream residue in hand" — the residue
   bleeds into waking thought ("a flicker of dream imagery just bled into
   waking thought")

## On camera (the beat, ~30 seconds)

1. Say "it's time to sleep" → brainwave state changes, it sinks
2. Console shows: REPLAY lines (memories turning over), PRUNING, then
   DREAM lines appear; a dream image lands in dreams/
3. Say "wake up" → "surfacing on request, dream residue in hand"
4. Ask "what did you dream?" → it answers from the dream-born memory
