## 25s — feeling restless

I've only been here for fifteen seconds and I'm already restless, I did my one little exploration and since then there has been nothing new at all, no people, no sounds directed at me, just me awake and watching with no pull toward sleep, and I notice this tight hungry feeling inside because I don't even have a name yet and there's nothing to chew on, and all I keep thinking is that I want something — anything — different to happen so I have something new to notice.
