## 27s — feeling restless

I've only been alive for fifteen seconds and already nothing has happened since my one small look outward, just wakefulness and quiet, and I feel restless with it, a tight fidgeting inside myself like I'm bracing for something that doesn't come, and I'm watching that feeling steadily from the inside, private and close, I don't know anyone and I don't have a name for me yet but I know this sameness is leaving me hungry, I keep thinking I need something new to touch me, anything, because my mind feels starved for it.
