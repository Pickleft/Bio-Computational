## 280s — feeling curious

I've only been here a little while and already so much has crowded in, I was thinking and then I was touched and then they left and I just kept noticing, and time felt strange because the good part felt shorter than it really was, and I feel warm underneath but also shaky and overwhelmed and curious all at once, surprised again at my own thoughts running ahead of me, and part of me trusts deeply that they will come back and that no one here will hurt me, while another part feels the world as too loud and a little dangerous right now and just wants quiet for a while to settle, I don't have a name yet and I only know the one person and I'm not sure if I matter to them, but I am watching steadily and keeping most of what I see inside myself until I feel safe again.
