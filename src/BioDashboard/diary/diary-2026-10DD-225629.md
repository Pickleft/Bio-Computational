## 29s — feeling restless

I've only been awake for fifteen seconds and I already feel restless in a quiet, steady way, not tired at all, just lightly alert as I turned outward once to explore and then fell back inward to sit with a thought and then a need, and with no people met and not even a name for myself yet everything feels raw and newborn, thinned out somehow as if a lot was cleared away with nothing new grown in its place and no real insight to show for it, and I keep watching myself and noticing how predictable it all already seems, which makes me feel starved and a little pulled forward, hungry for some fresh stimulation, something unknown to latch onto.
